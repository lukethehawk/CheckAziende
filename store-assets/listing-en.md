# CheckAziende — listing EN

## Name

CheckAziende

## Short description

Identifies the company behind the open website and shows public corporate and financial data.

## Full description

CheckAziende helps you quickly understand which company is associated with the website you are visiting.

The extension analyses the active page for public corporate information and, when available, displays:

- company name and activity status;
- VAT number, registered address, legal form and REA;
- ATECO industry code and description;
- revenue, profit/loss and employee count;
- EBITDA, EBITDA margin and other indicators when available;
- filed-account history;
- public email addresses and telephone numbers exposed by the open website.

Company data is verified and enriched through external public sources. Manual lookup by VAT number is also available.

CheckAziende contains no advertising, analytics or behavioral profiling and does not use its own remote backend.

Displayed information depends on the availability and freshness of public sources and is not a credit rating.
